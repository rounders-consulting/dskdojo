Traditional Shotokan Karate is built on three foundational pillars: kihon, kata, and kumite. Each one supports the others and creates a balanced, disciplined practice.

  • KihonFundamentals, stances, posture, and basic striking and blocking.
  • KataPrescribed forms that teach movement, timing, and technique application.
  • KumiteControlled partner exercises for practical application and reaction.
