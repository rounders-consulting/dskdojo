Downtown Shotokan Karate offers karate classes in Surrey, Langara karate classes, and Coquitlam martial arts classes for all experience levels. Whether you are looking for a class for a child, teen, adult, or beginner, our programs are structured to build confidence, discipline, and skill.

New to karate? Start here

Our Shotokan Karate classes are designed to be welcoming to beginners. Students begin with foundational movements, correct posture, balance, and focus before progressing into more advanced kata and partner work. This approach helps new students build confidence without feeling overwhelmed, while giving experienced students room to continue refining their technique.

For families, we offer a supportive environment where children, teens, and adults train with respect, patience, and clear guidance. Classes are listed for students age 7 through adults. Karate class questions

Are Shotokan Karate classes suitable for beginners?
Yes. Beginners are welcome. Classes start with foundational movement, posture, balance, and focus before progressing to more advanced kata and partner practice.
What ages can attend?
The schedule lists classes for students age 7 through adults. Each YMCA registration listing has the current program information for that location.
How do I register and find current program information?
Choose your dojo above and open its YMCA registration listing for current enrollment options and program details. For dojo addresses and phone numbers, visit the dojo directory.
What is taught in traditional Shotokan Karate?
Training includes kihon (fundamentals), kata (forms), and controlled kumite (partner practice). Read more in our Shotokan Karate techniques guide.
