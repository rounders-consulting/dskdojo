Rank
6th Dan “Rokudan” Black Belt
Dojo
Instructor, Langara Family YMCA

Sensei Eve McGrath first started in martial arts by studying Judo in university. She formally started as a white belt in DSK under Sensei Robert and James Lashin over twenty years ago.

She holds an ISKF “B” Official Judge certification. She is also a Provincial Referee under Karate BC, a provincial branch of Karate Canada and the World Karate Federation (WKF). She enjoys competing in both provincial and national tournaments.

Achievements and awards

  • 1st Place, Senior Women Individual Kata, 2012 ISKF Canadian Nationals
