Rank
7th Dan “Shichidan” Black Belt
Dojo
Chief Instructor, Surrey, Langara & Coquitlam YMCA dojos

Sensei Robert Lashin has been training in Shotokan Karate for over 42 years. He has also been a volunteer instructor with the club for over 20 years.

He continues his own training by attending seminars and training his students for various national and international tournaments. He holds an ISKF “B” Level Judge certification, is a graduate of the ISKF Instructor Trainee Program, and is a Certified Examiner.
