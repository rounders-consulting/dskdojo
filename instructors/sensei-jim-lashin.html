Rank
6th Dan “Rokudan” Black Belt
Dojo
Instructor, Langara Family YMCA

Sensei James Lashin started his training in karate 40 years ago. He has been a volunteer instructor with the YMCA since 1980.

He continues his own training by attending yearly Master camps and seminars. He also acts as a judge at national and international tournaments and holds an ISKF “B” Level Judge certification.
