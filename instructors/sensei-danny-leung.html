Rank
6th Dan Black Belt
Dojo
Chief Instructor, Langara Family YMCA
Qualifications
ISKF Level C Official; National Coaching Certification Program, Level 2

Sensei Danny Leung started his karate training at UBC in 1983. The UBC club was associated with the Shotokan group at the YMCA, and he has been with the YMCA ever since. Danny has been a volunteer instructor at the YMCA dojos since 1991.

Sensei Danny Leung is valued for his detail-oriented instruction and his effort to maintain technical quality across the training environment. He encourages students to focus on precision, discipline, and consistency, reinforcing the importance of fundamentals in every class.
